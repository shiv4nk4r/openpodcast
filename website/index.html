What we’ve built

A Riverside-style flow that stays on your machines — from lobby check to Premiere timeline.

Local HQ recording

Host and up to three guests each capture camera and mic locally. No streamed “master” that dies if someone’s Wi‑Fi dips.

Mic test before join

Hear exactly what the studio is picking up before you enter — check level and clarity in the lobby, then join when it sounds right.

Voice profiles

Studio-tuned EQ presets (Broadcast, Warm, Bright, Cut rumble, Deep, High, and Flat) apply to your mic test, what peers hear, and the recorded tracks.

Encrypted peer sync

Join with an invite ticket and studio password. Live traffic rides iroh over QUIC — encrypted in transit, direct on LAN or via relay.

Live studio stage

Roster, presence, preview frames, and screen share so the booth feels like you’re in the same room while files stay local.

Echo control

Native AEC3 on the live path, with a simple headphones check so speaker feedback doesn’t wreck the take.

Editor & layouts

Playback with presence-aware layouts, then compose a final cut or hand off to your NLE.

Premiere XML export

Ship a Final Cut Pro 7 XMEML package so Adobe Premiere can open your multi-track timeline with media linked.

On-device captions

Optional native speech-to-text on supported platforms — captions without uploading the episode to a third-party API.

How a session runs

Four beats from empty booth to editable tracks.

  1. Host a studio Create a session, set a password, share the invite.
  2. Check cam & mic Test pickup, pick a voice profile, then admit guests — max four total.
  3. Record locally Everyone rolls on their own disk; you still hear each other live.
  4. Sync & export Tracks return to the host over P2P. Preview, render, or export XML.
